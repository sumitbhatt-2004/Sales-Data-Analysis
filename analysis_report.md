# 4. Key Insights & Recommendations
1) Total Revenue: The total revenue generated from sales is 12,365,048. This indicates the overall performance of the sales team and the effectiveness of current marketing strategies.

2) Average Revenue per Sale: The average revenue per sale is 12,360.48. This metric helps in understanding the average value of each transaction and can be effectively utilized to set future sales targets.

3) Top Products: The top 5 products with the highest sales are Laptop, Tablet, Phone, Headphones, Monitor. This information can be leveraged to focus marketing efforts on high-demand products and optimize inventory.

4) Regional Performance: The best-performing regions with the highest sales are North and South. This insight can guide resource allocation and regional marketing investments toward the most profitable areas.
